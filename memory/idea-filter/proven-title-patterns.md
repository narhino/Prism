# Proven title patterns (from competitor outlier analysis)

## High-performing patterns observed
- "[Famous event] Explained Like You're 5" — Crayon Capital
- "Who really [controls / profits from / killed] X?" — Abundantia
- "The [thing] LIE" / "The [thing] is a SCAM" — Casual Finance, Abundantia
- "[Specific person/company] [verb of dominance] the world" — Abundantia BlackRock
- "WTF Just Happened To [X]?!" — Nick Invests, Casual Finance
- "The [year] [event] Nobody Remembers" — Casual Finance
- "How You Are PROGRAMMED to [bad outcome]" — Abundantia
- "Brace For Impact." (short, ominous) — Casual Finance
- "Something Feels Off About [year]…" — Casual Finance

## Title rules
- One name in the title (institution OR person OR year)
- Curiosity gap — never reveal the answer
- Negative emotional word OK: lie, scam, evil, trap, dead, killed, programmed
- 6–10 words ideal
- Sentence case, not Title Case
