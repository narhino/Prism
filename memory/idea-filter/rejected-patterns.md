# Rejected patterns

## Format-level rejections (always)
- Generic "How to invest in X" titles
- "5 things I learned" listicles
- Pure prediction titles ("Stock market will crash in 2027") *unless the prediction itself is the news*

## Lawsuit guardrail
- Don't name a specific living **private** individual as the central villain unless the claim is already public record. Public figures, officials, executives, and corporations are fair game.

## What is NOT rejected
- Partisan framing — allowed
- Hot takes / sharp opinions — allowed
- Market calls — allowed
- Financial-advice-adjacent angles — allowed
- The grey area is the point, not the exception.
