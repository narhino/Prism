# Scenes — Saudi Arabia sells dollars, not oil.

## Character library

**Henry Kissinger:** A heavy-set man in his early fifties, broad shoulders, thick dark-rimmed glasses, slicked-back dark hair, wearing a dark wide-lapel 1970s suit with a wide tie. Heavy jowls, prominent forehead.

**Paul Volcker:** An exceptionally tall, lanky man in his early fifties, balding on top with a thin ring of hair around the sides, wearing a rumpled 1970s suit, often holding a cigar. Long face, hooded eyes.

**American driver (Ohio):** A middle-aged man in a baseball cap, plaid shirt and jeans, slight gut, work boots. Holds a gas-pump nozzle in one hand. Average build, weary posture.

**Saudi official:** A bearded man in traditional white thobe and red-and-white checkered ghutra headdress secured by a black agal, mid-forties, calm bearing.

**US Treasury official:** A clean-shaven man in his fifties in a modern dark business suit, white shirt, plain tie, neat side-parted graying hair, carrying a leather folder under one arm.
