## 5. Publishing notes

**Recommended slot:** Tuesday or Wednesday, 1:00–3:00 PM ET.

Reasoning:
- Long-form finance/macro browse traffic peaks weekday afternoons in U.S. time zones; weekend posts compete with entertainment and underperform on session contribution.
- Tuesday 1 PM ET gives the algorithm 36 hours of weekday browse cycles before the weekend, which 2026 research consistently flags as where satisfaction scores accumulate fastest for long-form (satisfaction now outweighs raw watch time in the 2026 algorithm).
- Avoid Monday — macro/CPI news cycle dominates Monday surfaces and crowds out evergreen explainer traffic. Avoid Friday — session contribution drops over weekends.

**Audience targeting:**
- Primary: macro/finance long-form viewers (Crayon Capital, Casual Finance, Abundantia overlap). They'll find this via Browse and Suggested off any oil/inflation video they've watched in the past 7 days.
- Secondary: news-followers searching "Hormuz" or "oil shock" — they will NOT find this title in search. That's the deliberate trade-off in our title rubric. The thumbnail recovers them via Browse.
- Set the video's language to English and the category to **Education** (not "News & Politics") — Education is where the evergreen recommendation cluster lives and matches our 18-month shelf-life goal.

**Pinned-comment suggestion (optional):** A one-liner restating the campfire premise — *"Every American gas pump is a Treasury auction in disguise."* Pinned comments are not CTAs and don't violate the description rules; they reliably lift the comment-engagement satisfaction signal.
