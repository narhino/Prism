# Concept — Saudi Arabia sells dollars, not oil.

## Final title

**Saudi Arabia sells dollars, not oil.**

## Title variants tested

1. *Who really profits when Hormuz closes?* — original Idea Filter working title. Cut: too soft, news-pegged, expires when the strait reopens. Loses the entire thesis.
2. *Hormuz closed. So why is Riyadh winning?* — hybrid news + curiosity gap. Cut: still shelves the moment the strait reopens. Carries the news but not the mechanism.
3. **Saudi Arabia sells dollars, not oil.** — chosen. Contradiction-stack pattern (Crayon Capital outlier model). Six words, lowercase, one named entity, evergreen. Still true in six months when Hormuz reopens. Delivers the entire thesis upfront.

## Thumbnail concept

Hand-drawn on a real whiteboard, black dry-erase marker, photographed straight-on at 16:9. Center: a top-down map of the Strait of Hormuz drawn as two narrowing coastlines tightening into a chokepoint, with tiny labels "IRAN" (north) and "OMAN" (south) and a single oil tanker silhouette inside the choke. Top third: large bold all-caps hand-lettering reading **DOLLAR WILL SURVIVE**, with SURVIVE underlined by one firm marker stroke. Bottom-left corner: a faintly-erased ghost of an oil barrel, only partly visible — implying the "oil" answer was wiped away. A thin curving arrow exits the strait, travels across the board, and ends in a small "$" symbol near the right edge.

The news is pegged in the image (Hormuz, the live event). The thesis is in the overlay text. Together they read: *this week's headline is the dollar machine still running.* Title and thumbnail complete each other rather than repeat.

**Style note (open decision):** executed as literal black marker on a whiteboard rather than the brand-bible's white-line-on-charcoal. House style needs to be locked before scene work — currently held for user decision.
