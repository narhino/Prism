# Reference channels

What we copy, what we differentiate from.

## Abundantia (~732K subs, 52.7M views)

- **Same as us:** trigger → evergreen formula.
- **Top hit:** "BlackRock owns the world" (9.65M views).
- **We differ in:** tone — calmer, less ranty, more "follow the receipts" than "wake up sheeple."

## Casual Finance (~191K subs, 8M views)

- **Same as us:** trigger → evergreen formula, lowercase casual tone.
- **Top hits:** "Brace For Impact" (1.98M), "1973 Crash Nobody Remembers" (1.24M).
- **We differ in:** scope — not just markets. Wars, disasters, scandals, IPOs, sports, geopolitics.

## Crayon Capital (~211K subs, 15M views)

- **Same as us:** ELI5 history of finance.
- **Top hit:** "Wolf of Wall Street" (4.09M).
- **We differ in:** style — white-on-dark, not crayon. Dual-time: every video shows past + present side by side.

## Nick Invests (~173K subs, 15.2M views)

- **Same as us:** finance-explainer animation.
- **Their focus:** personal-finance traps.
- **We differ in:** framing — institutional/mechanism focus, not lifestyle. We don't talk to viewers about their own money. We show them how the system works.
